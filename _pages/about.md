Welcome! I'm Iker, a PhD student in Data-Driven Engineering & Sciences at the University of Bristol. I enjoy working where mathematics, learning and decision-making meet, and finding connections between ideas from different fields.

## What I research

My research focuses on **[uncertainty quantification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uncertainty_quantification) in [multi-agent systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-agent_system)**. I am interested in how learning systems make decisions under uncertainty, particularly when several agents interact and influence one another, as in [multi-agent reinforcement learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-agent_reinforcement_learning). This has to do with [collective intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collective_intelligence) in a broad sense, and is one of the most interesting, real-world applied things I have delved into.

More broadly, my interests include **[sequential decision-making](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sequential_decision_making), [statistical learning theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_learning_theory), [imprecise probabilities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imprecise_probability), [Bayesian statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayesian_statistics) and [Dempster–Shafer theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dempster%E2%80%93Shafer_theory)**. The questions that draw me in are how we can understand what a model does and does not know, and how that uncertainty should shape the decisions we make with it, particularly when many agents are present.

My background is a bit of a mess: economics, statistics and mathematics, spanning one BSc and 3 different MScs. However, I firmly believe that in this new era, multidisciplinarity is the only way value will be created. This also helped me to work in different financial firms and at the intersection of different topics, from machine learning to stochastic calculus. I remain interested in the connections between my background areas, though. You can find more about my background in my [CV]({{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}).

## Beyond research

I'm also a bit of an **economics and finance nerd**, but especially enthusiastic about **education** and all technological developments in it. I enjoy thinking about how people learn and how technology can help with learning: so I am interested in how learning helps us learn.

I also spend time on **science communication and educational outreach** through social media (STILL IN PROCESS!): explaining ideas, sharing what I learn and making technical topics more approachable. Whether the subject is mathematics, economics or finance, I love a good explanation and the conversations it can start. I try to get to all audiences I can, especially the Spanish and Chinese audience (too much content in English is created!), but I normally create content in English as well.

If you would like to talk about research, education, or an interesting idea in economics or finance, [get in touch](mailto:{{ site.author.contact_email }})!
