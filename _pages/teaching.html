January–March 2027

I will be teaching a course on sequential decision-making to Key Stage 4 pupils at City Academy Bristol, in Easton, Bristol.

The programme focuses on widening access to university for pupils from less advantaged backgrounds and groups underrepresented in higher education. I want these tutorials to give pupils a chance to ask questions, try out new ideas and get a feel for university-style learning.

Sequential decision-making

Exploring how the choices we make now affect future possibilities, and how to reason about decisions when outcomes are uncertain.

Research & university access

Making research approachable for Key Stage 4 pupils, encouraging curiosity and helping them see higher education as a possibility.
